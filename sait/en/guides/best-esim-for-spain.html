Which eSIM to get for Spain: mobile data from the minute you land

An eSIM is a SIM card without the plastic. You buy it online, install it on your phone with a QR code, and your data works as soon as you land — no roaming charges and no hunting for a phone shop at the airport.

Does your phone support eSIM

Most recent iPhone, Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel models do. To check:

  • iPhone: Settings → Mobile Data (Cellular) → you should see "Add eSIM"
  • Android: Settings → Connections → SIM manager → "Add eSIM"
Your phone must be unlocked. If you got it on contract from a carrier, check this before you travel

When to buy and how to install

  1. Buy the data plan at home, before your trip
  2. Scan the QR code from the email — the eSIM installs in about a minute
  3. Switch it on when you land in Spain: the plan usually starts counting from the first connection
  4. Keep your main SIM on for calls and bank text messages, and use the eSIM for data

How much data you need

Compare plans by gigabytes and validity. A small plan is usually enough for a one-week holiday; for a month with maps, video calls and social media, take a bigger one or an unlimited plan. If you are also visiting Portugal, France or Italy, a Europe-wide plan saves you buying a new one at every border.

eSIM or a Spanish SIM card

  • holiday or a few weeks in Spain — an eSIM is simpler: everything is ready before you leave, no paperwork, no shops
  • moving to Spain — you will need a local SIM with a Spanish number: banks, government offices and appointment systems send their codes by text message to a Spanish number
