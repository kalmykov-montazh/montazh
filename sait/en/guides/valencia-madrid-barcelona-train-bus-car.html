Valencia to Madrid or Barcelona: train, bus, car or plane

Valencia is well connected: Madrid is about two hours away by high-speed train and Barcelona about three. Here is how the options compare.

Valencia — Madrid

  • High-speed train: about 1 hour 50 minutes to 2 hours. Four companies compete on this route — Renfe AVE, Renfe Avlo (low-cost), Iryo and Ouigo — so prices drop a lot if you book early. In Valencia trains leave from Joaquín Sorolla station; in Madrid most of them arrive at Chamartín, not Atocha, so check your station
  • Bus: about 4–4.5 hours. Cheaper than a last-minute train, but slower
  • Car: about 350 km, roughly 3.5 hours on the A-3 motorway, no tolls
  • Plane: flight time is under an hour, but with airport transfers and security it is slower than the train
Low-cost trains (Avlo, Ouigo) charge extra for a large suitcase if you do not add it when booking. Adding it at the station is always more expensive

Valencia — Barcelona

  • Train: about 3 hours (Euromed and other services along the coast)
  • Bus: around 4.5–5 hours
  • Car: about 350 km along the coast; part of the route is on the AP-7

When a car is the better choice

A rental car makes sense if you travel as a family or group, want to stop in towns along the way (Cuenca on the way to Madrid, Peñíscola or Tarragona on the way to Barcelona), or continue to places trains do not reach. Compare prices for the whole trip: for three or four people, one car often costs less than four train tickets.

How to pay less

  • book trains two to six weeks ahead — the cheapest fares go first
  • compare all four train companies, not only Renfe
  • for a car, pick it up in the city instead of the airport if you do not need it on arrival day
