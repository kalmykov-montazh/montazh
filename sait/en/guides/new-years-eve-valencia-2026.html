New Year's Eve in Valencia 2026: the 12 grapes, fireworks and where to stay

Valencia doesn't do a quiet New Year's Eve. The city that burns giant statues every March sees in the new year with gunpowder, music and thousands of people packed into one square.

Where the party is

The heart of it is Plaza del Ayuntamiento, the big square in front of City Hall. Last year the night party ran from 22:30 to 01:45, with DJs, big screens and confetti.

At midnight the City Hall clock strikes twelve, and on every strike you eat one grape. Twelve grapes, twelve wishes, one for each month. Locals buy small tins of peeled, seedless grapes in any supermarket from mid-December. Grab one: chewing whole grapes against the clock is harder than it sounds.

Two firework shows at once

Last year Valencia ran two shows at the same moment:

  • a 90-second burst from the City Hall terrace, right over the square, timed with the chimes;
  • a 9-minute firework "castle" over the City of Arts and Sciences, the futuristic white buildings in the old riverbed.

If crowds aren't your thing, the City of Arts and Sciences is the better pick: more space, a wider view, and the show is much longer. The 2026 programme is usually announced by the city in late December, so check the details closer to the date.

With kids

On the morning of 31 December the square hosts "kids' chimes": a countdown at noon, confetti and music, so families get the whole experience without staying up till midnight.

Where to stay

New Year's Eve plus the Christmas holidays is high season, and the closer you get the fewer central rooms are left.

  • Ciutat Vella (old town): walk to the square in 5 minutes and back to bed after the party. The most expensive and the first to sell out.
  • Ruzafa (Russafa): 10–15 minutes on foot, full of bars and restaurants, livelier and usually cheaper than the old town.
  • Near the City of Arts and Sciences: modern hotels, quieter streets, front-row seat for the big firework show.
  • Cabanyal / the beach: the cheapest option and lovely in the daytime, but plan your way back: after midnight taxis are scarce.

Getting there and around

From the airport to the centre is about 20–25 minutes by metro (lines 3 and 5) or taxi. Book a transfer in advance if you land late on the 31st: everyone is heading the same way. Get an eSIM before you fly so you have maps and messages the minute you land.
