Driving in Spain with a UK or US licence: what you need as a visitor and as a resident

The rules are different for tourists and for people who live in Spain, and different for UK and US licences.

Visiting Spain

  • UK licence: the UK government says visitors with a valid UK photocard licence do not need an International Driving Permit (IDP) to drive in Spain
  • US licence: the US State Department advises US citizens to get an International Driving Permit before arriving and carry it together with their US licence and passport. Some rental desks do not ask for it, but the police can, and driving without it can mean a fine

Always carry your licence, passport and the rental documents in the car.

Moving to Spain

Once you become a resident, a foreign licence is only valid for a limited time:

  • UK licence: valid for six months after you become resident. Within that time you can exchange it for a Spanish licence without a test, thanks to the UK–Spain agreement
  • US licence: also valid for six months after residency, but Spain and the US have no exchange agreement, so after that you need to get a Spanish licence with theory and practical tests
The exchange and the tests are handled by the DGT (Spain's traffic authority). Appointments can take weeks, so start early

What rental companies check

  • a licence held for at least one or two years, depending on the company
  • extra fees for drivers under 25 at many companies
  • a credit card in the main driver's name for the deposit — how to rent without one

Read the rental conditions for your exact booking before you pay: the accepted licences and the IDP requirement are listed there
