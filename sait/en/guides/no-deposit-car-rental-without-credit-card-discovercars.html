No deposit car rental without a credit card with DiscoverCars: how it works (2026–2027)

You can now rent a car with no security deposit and no credit card at all, in Spain and in many other countries. DiscoverCars has a booking type for this called No Deposit: you prepay the rental online in full, and at the desk you only show your driving licence and passport. Nothing is blocked on your card, because nobody asks for a card.

This guide covers how to find these cars, what you need at pick-up, what you are still responsible for, and the mistakes first-time renters make most often.

In short: search on DiscoverCars, tick the "No Deposit" or "No Card Required" filter and book a fully prepaid offer. At the desk: licence, passport or ID and your voucher. No deposit, no card

Why rental companies usually want a credit card and a deposit

A security deposit is an amount the rental company blocks on your card for the length of the rental. It protects the company against damage, fines or a car returned without a full tank. In Spain deposits range from €200–300 to €1,000–1,500 or more, depending on the company and the car class.

Blocking money is easiest on a credit card, so many companies write "credit card in the main driver's name only" in their terms. Debit cards are often refused, and when they are accepted the money usually leaves your account straight away and comes back later — sometimes weeks later.

That is why the classic airport story goes like this: the car is paid for, but the desk says it cannot be released without a credit card, or only with their own expensive insurance. More on this in Renting a car in Spain without a credit card

What No Deposit on DiscoverCars actually is

DiscoverCars compares prices from hundreds of rental companies. Some offers now carry a Deposit Free badge. It means:

  • no security deposit is taken at the desk
  • no payment card is needed to collect the car
  • your money stays available for the whole trip
  • instead of your deposit, DiscoverCars guarantees to the rental company that eligible charges will be settled

The option works only with fully prepaid bookings: you pay for the whole rental online when you book. You can pay with a debit card — which is the whole point: you do not need a credit card at any stage.

The price shown in the search results already includes the option. It is preselected when you book, but you can remove it and take a standard offer with a deposit if that works out cheaper for you.

How to find a no deposit car, step by step

  1. Open DiscoverCars and enter the city or airport, dates and driver's age
  2. Find the filters in the results: on the left on desktop, at the top on mobile
  3. Tick "No Deposit" and/or "No Card Required"
  4. Only offers with no deposit and no card at pick-up will remain
  5. Compare supplier ratings, fuel policy and pick-up location (in the terminal or by shuttle)
  6. Book and pay the full amount online — with a debit card or any other card
  7. Keep the voucher on your phone and, just in case, print it

For Valencia there is a ready-made page with these offers at the airport: no deposit car rental at Valencia Airport

These offers are not available everywhere or on every date. Big tourist airports usually have more, small towns may have none. If the filter shows nothing, try a nearby airport or different dates

What you need at the rental desk

For a No Deposit booking the desk will ask for:

  • your driving licence
  • your passport or ID card
  • your booking voucher (on your phone or on paper)
  • in some countries, an International Driving Permit if your licence was not issued in the EU

You sign the rental agreement as usual and get the keys. No card comes out, no money is blocked.

UK and US drivers should also check the rules for their licence in Spain before the trip — see Driving in Spain with a UK or US licence

What you are still responsible for

No Deposit removes the deposit, not the responsibility. This is the most important thing to understand before you travel.

Damage. You are liable for damage up to the excess (deductible) set by the rental company. No Deposit does not change the excess — the money is simply not blocked in advance.

Fuel, tolls, traffic fines, cleaning. These stay yours under the rental agreement. Even full insurance does not cover them.

Charges found later. If a fine or toll shows up after you return the car, DiscoverCars contacts you directly and settles it with you. The rental company cannot take money from your card — it does not have it.

Something found at return. You can settle with the rental company on the spot, but you do not have to — it can go through DiscoverCars.

No Deposit is not insurance. It is a way to rent without a deposit. Insurance is a separate add-on, covered below

Full Coverage: do you need it with No Deposit?

Full Coverage is DiscoverCars' own protection, added when you book. It works as reimbursement: if something happens, eligible costs are paid back to you within the policy limits.

The key difference:

  • with a standard booking (with a deposit) Full Coverage does not remove the deposit. The rental company still blocks money on your credit card, charges you first if there is damage, and you then file a claim with DiscoverCars. Claims must be filed within 90 days of returning the car
  • with a No Deposit booking damage covered by Full Coverage is settled by DiscoverCars directly with the rental company

That is why DiscoverCars itself recommends pairing No Deposit with Full Coverage: no deposit, and no exposure to the excess.

What the desk will try to sell you

Even with a no deposit booking, the agent may offer their own insurance or "zero excess" package. It is optional. If you already have Full Coverage, you can politely decline — it does not affect your booking.

Sometimes the desk says "DiscoverCars insurance is not valid". Usually that only means they cannot see it in their system, because it is separate reimbursement cover, not their own policy. It still works.

Before you sign, check the lines for optional insurance or waivers — they should say "declined" or €0.00 if you did not buy them.

Extras bought at the desk — child seat, additional driver, GPS, cross-border permit, extending the rental — are paid directly to the rental company. You may need a card or another payment method for those, even on a no deposit booking

No Deposit vs a standard booking with a debit card

There is a third route: some companies accept a debit card for the deposit, but only if you buy their insurance at the desk. The deposit is then lower or zero, but the daily price goes up noticeably. And the same company may have different rules at different airports.

A simple comparison:

  • standard booking + credit card — lowest rental price, but you need a credit card with enough limit for the deposit
  • standard booking + debit card — not every company accepts it, often with compulsory desk insurance and a slow deposit refund
  • No Deposit — no card at the desk, no deposit, everything prepaid; the price can be a little higher than a standard offer, but there are no surprises at pick-up

If you have no credit card, or simply do not want €1,000 frozen during your holiday, No Deposit is the calmest option.

Common mistakes

  • Booking the cheapest offer without the filter and finding out at the desk that a credit card in the driver's name is required
  • Mixing up Full Coverage and no deposit: thinking that with insurance there will be no deposit. With a standard booking there will be
  • Not reading the fuel policy. The clearest one is "full to full"
  • Not filming the car at pick-up. Record all sides, wheels, glass, interior and the fuel gauge — three minutes that prevent arguments at return
  • Forgetting that child seats, a second driver and cross-border travel are paid separately at the desk

FAQ

Can I rent a car in Spain without a credit card?

Yes. The simplest way is a No Deposit booking on DiscoverCars: the rental is prepaid online in full and no card is requested at the desk

Can I pay for a No Deposit booking with a debit card?

Yes. Payment happens online when you book, and a debit card works for that. No card is needed at the desk to collect the car

If there is no deposit, am I responsible for nothing?

No. Damage up to the excess, fuel, tolls, fines and cleaning are still yours. To avoid the excess risk, add Full Coverage to a No Deposit booking

Does Full Coverage remove the deposit on a standard booking?

No. On a standard booking the rental company still blocks a deposit on your credit card, and Full Coverage reimburses you afterwards if something happens. Only No Deposit offers have no deposit

What should I bring to pick up the car?

Your driving licence, passport or ID and the booking voucher. If your licence was not issued in the EU, some countries also require an International Driving Permit

What happens if a fine arrives after I return the car?

DiscoverCars contacts you directly and settles the fine with you. The rental company cannot charge your card because it never had it

Are no deposit cars available everywhere?

No, only where suppliers offer the option. They are most common at large tourist airports. If the filter is empty, try a nearby airport or different dates
