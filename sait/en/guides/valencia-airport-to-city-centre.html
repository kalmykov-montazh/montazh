Valencia Airport to the city centre: metro, bus, taxi or private transfer

Valencia Airport (Manises) is about 8 km from the city centre. You have four ways to get into town: metro, bus, taxi or a transfer booked in advance.

Metro — fast and cheap

Metro lines 3 and 5 run from the airport into the city. The station is on the lower floor of the terminal.

  • trains every 15–20 minutes
  • about 20 minutes to the centre
  • handy stops in the centre: Xàtiva (next to the Nord railway station) and Colón
  • a single ticket costs around €4.80, plus the rechargeable Móbilis card itself (€1.10–2.10), which you can top up later
The airport is a separate fare zone, so the ticket costs more than a normal city ride. Check the price at the ticket machine — fares change

Bus 150

Metrobus line 150 goes from the airport to the centre via Mislata, Quart de Poblet and Manises. It is slower than the metro, about 45 minutes, and you can buy the ticket from the driver.

Taxi

The taxi rank is right outside arrivals. A ride to the centre usually costs €20–25, depending on the time of day and the exact street. Airport trips have a minimum fare and a pick-up supplement, so even a short ride is not cheap.

Fixed-price transfer

If you land late at night, travel with children or have lots of luggage, a pre-booked transfer is the easiest option. The driver waits for you with a name sign, and the price is fixed before the trip, whatever the traffic.

Which one to choose

  • travelling light during the day — metro
  • family, lots of bags, late flight — transfer or taxi
