Madrid Christmas lights 2026 are on: dates, hours and where to see them

Madrid switched on its Christmas lights on Saturday 21 November 2026 with a show in Plaza de Cibeles, and they will stay up until 6 January 2027, Three Kings Day. If you are planning a December city break or flying in for New Year, the whole centre is already lit and the season runs for about seven weeks.

This year the city has decorated more than 250 spots across all 21 districts, with over 14 million LED bulbs and about 164 km of light chains. There are 131 illuminated cherry trees and 14 big lit fir trees. For the third year running, some designs on the streets were drawn by local primary school children, and Spanish fashion designers from ACME are behind several of the canopies, including those on Gran Vía and Calle de Serrano. Plaza de Cibeles also gets a large Christmas tree.

What it means for you

The lights come on at 18:00 every evening. From Sunday to Thursday they go off at midnight, on Fridays and Saturdays at 01:00. On a few nights they stay on longer: until 03:00 on 24 December and 5 January, and until 06:00 on New Year's Eve. The city says these times are still provisional, so check again closer to your dates.

The busiest evenings are weekends and the long weekend of 5–8 December, when Spaniards from other regions come to Madrid. The 7th is a public holiday in the Madrid region this year. Hotels in Sol, Gran Vía and around Cibeles fill up early on those dates, so if you want to walk out of your door and be in the middle of it, book now rather than in December.

The easy route on foot is Cibeles, Gran Vía, Callao, Sol and on to Plaza Mayor, where the Christmas market opens on 27 November. Allow about two hours with stops. The city's double-decker Christmas bus, Naviluz, does a loop past the main streets with fixed departure times, and tickets sell out, so book on the official Naviluz site before you go.

Metro runs until about 01:30, which covers most nights. On 24 and 31 December plan your way back in advance, as taxis are hard to find after midnight
