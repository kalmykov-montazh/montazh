Benidorm is about 50 km north of Alicante-Elche Airport, a 40–50 minute drive. The cheapest way there is the direct Alsa bus, the most comfortable is a taxi or a transfer booked in advance, and the tram only makes sense if you are stopping in Alicante first.

## Direct Alsa bus — the best value

Alsa runs a direct coach from the airport to Benidorm bus station.

- departures roughly every hour, from about 06:00 to 23:00
- the ride takes 45–55 minutes
- the bus stop is on the departures level, follow the "Autobuses" signs
- a single ticket costs roughly €8–10 (November 2026); third-party sites quote slightly different fares, so check the price on the Alsa website or app
- a cabin bag and one large suitcase are included

In Benidorm most buses stop at the main bus station, and some also stop on Avenida de Europa. Look at which stop is closer to your hotel: from the bus station to the Levante beach hotels you may still need a short taxi.

> In summer and on Friday evenings buses fill up. Buy your ticket online the day before so you are not left waiting for the next one

## Bus C-6 plus TRAM — only if you have time

There is no tram station at the airport. You first take the C-6 airport bus into Alicante (around €4.50, every 20 minutes or so) to Luceros or Mercado, then change to TRAM line 1 towards Benidorm.

The tram part alone takes 70–80 minutes. In total you pay a little less than the direct bus but spend about twice as long, with luggage and a change. It works well if you want a few hours in Alicante on the way, not straight after a flight.

## Taxi

The taxi rank is outside arrivals on the ground floor and works 24 hours.

- the ride takes 35–45 minutes
- expect roughly €70–90 (November 2026), with higher fares at night, at weekends and on public holidays
- there is an airport supplement on top of the meter

Official taxis are white. Ignore drivers who approach you inside the terminal and offer a "good price".

## Private transfer — fixed price, no surprises

A transfer booked in advance costs about the same as a taxi, roughly €70 for a car with up to four people (November 2026). The difference is that the price is fixed, the driver tracks your flight and waits in arrivals with your name on a sign.

This is the easiest choice if:

- your flight lands after 22:00 and the last bus may already be gone
- you travel with children and need child seats
- there are four or more of you, so the price per person is close to the bus

Shared shuttles are another middle option: around €10 per person, but they stop at several hotels, so the trip can take up to 90 minutes.

## Rental car

If you plan day trips to Altea, Guadalest or Valencia, it is cheaper to pick up a car at the airport than to pay for a taxi there and back. Parking in central Benidorm is tight, so check whether your hotel has spaces before you book.

## Which one to choose

- travelling light on a budget — direct Alsa bus
- late arrival, kids, or a group of four — private transfer
- day trips planned — rental car from the airport
- no rush and want to see Alicante — C-6 bus plus tram

Booking the ride before you fly is what saves the most nerves: after a delayed flight at midnight, a driver already waiting with your name is worth a lot
