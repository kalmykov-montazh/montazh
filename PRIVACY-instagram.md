# Privacy Policy — kalmykov-bot (Instagram)

Last updated: 7 October 2026

kalmykov-bot is a private tool used only by Ievgen Kalmykov (Kalmykov Realty, Valencia, Spain) to manage comments and messages on his own Instagram account @kalmykov.rieltor.valencia.

**What data is processed.** Public comments left under posts of @kalmykov.rieltor.valencia, the commenter's Instagram username, and direct messages sent to this account.

**How it is used.** Only to reply to these comments and messages on behalf of the account owner. Comment text may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic Claude API) to draft a reply. The data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

**Storage.** The tool stores only technical identifiers of comments it has already processed. No profiles or personal databases are created.

**Data deletion.** To request deletion of any data related to you, write a direct message to @kalmykov.rieltor.valencia on Instagram or email evgeniy03091982@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.
